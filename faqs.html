Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers about document review, timelines, and Colombian visa advisory support.

Answers to common questions

What does document review include?

Document review focuses on completeness, consistency, and clarity against your checklist and application context.

What is included in the Consultation service?

The $64 consultation includes a focused session of up to 45 minutes, a discussion of your visa objective and possible pathway, and a personalized summary of practical next steps.

Not included: document-by-document review, application form completion, submission, government fees, translations, apostilles, legalizations, or guaranteed eligibility or approval.

What is included in Document Review?

The $176 service includes a checklist-based review of the documents you provide, notes about missing or inconsistent information, and a written action plan for improving the application package.

Not included: obtaining documents for you, sworn translations, apostilles, legalizations, government fees, form completion, submission, or follow-up with authorities.

What is included in Application Readiness?

The $283 service includes everything in Document Review, plus a cross-check of your application information and final guidance to help you prepare a complete submission-ready package.

Not included: government fees, third-party document services, translations, apostilles, legalizations, medical exams, insurance, application submission on your behalf, or a guarantee of approval.

What is included in Full Application Support?

The $494 service includes everything in Application Readiness, support completing the online application form, document organization for submission, submission guidance, and initial follow-up guidance after filing.

Not included: official visa fees, translations, apostilles, legalizations, insurance, medical examinations, courier charges, unrelated legal or tax services, or control over the authority's review, requests, timeline, or decision.

When do I pay for ColombiaVisa services?

Unless a written quote says otherwise, professional service fees are paid in full before work begins. Government visa fees and third-party expenses are separate and are paid directly to the relevant authority or provider.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Depending on your location and currency, payment may be available through PayPal, credit or debit card, Bre-B, bank transfer, Nequi, or Wise transfer for international clients once the account is available. We will confirm the available option and payment instructions before you pay.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. Approval decisions are made solely by Colombian authorities based on their evaluation criteria.

How long does the process usually take?

Timing varies by case complexity and official processing periods. You will receive guidance on realistic preparation timelines.

What should I prepare before the consultation?

Bring your visa objective, current document set, and any questions about your timeline or case details.

Which Colombian visa category may fit my situation?

The right category depends on your purpose in Colombia, personal circumstances, and supporting documents. We can help you organize the relevant facts before you review the official requirements.

Do foreign documents need an apostille or legalization?

Many foreign public documents require an apostille or legalization, depending on the issuing country and document type. Confirm the current requirement for your case before submitting.

Do my documents need to be translated into Spanish?

Documents issued in another language may need an official Spanish translation. The exact translation standard can vary by document and application, so check the current instructions for your visa category.

Can digital nomads apply for a Colombian visa?

Digital nomad applicants may have a dedicated route when they meet the applicable eligibility and income requirements. We can help you prepare the information needed to assess your next steps.

Can I apply for a visa while I am already in Colombia?

This depends on the visa category, your current status, and the rules in effect when you apply. Review the official process and your permitted stay before making plans.

How can I avoid delays in my application?

Use current forms, provide complete and consistent information, prepare readable supporting documents, and address apostille or translation requirements before submission.

Can you help with visa renewals or changes of category?

We can review your current situation and documents for readiness. Eligibility, deadlines, and available procedures are determined by Colombian immigration authorities.

Does ColombiaVisa make the approval decision?

No. ColombiaVisa provides independent preparation and document-review support. Only the competent Colombian authority can evaluate and decide an application.
