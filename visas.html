Colombian Visa Types

Understand the main visa categories and identify the path that best fits your plans in Colombia.

Choose the category that fits your journey

Migrant Visa (Type M)

For foreigners establishing a qualifying relationship, activity, or long-term purpose in Colombia.

Visitor Visa (Type V)

For temporary visits, remote work for foreign companies, business, events, and other authorized activities.

Resident Visa (Type R)

For qualifying investors, parents of Colombian nationals, and applicants who meet the applicable residence pathway.

Important: Requirements depend on your nationality, location, document origin, and the visa category. The Colombian Ministry of Foreign Affairs makes the final decision. Confirm the current rules on the official visa portal before submitting.

Common application requirements

  • Completed online visa application and a valid passport or travel document.
  • Recent digital photograph that meets the application specifications.
  • Supporting evidence for the selected category and proof of the purpose of travel or stay.
  • Documents issued abroad that are apostilled or legalized and translated into Spanish when required.
  • Additional evidence requested by the visa authority, including proof of funds, insurance, or civil-status documents when applicable.

Visa-specific requirements

Visitor Visa (Type V)

Tourism

  • Passport and application information.
  • Evidence of the travel purpose, itinerary, accommodation, and sufficient funds when requested.
  • Return or onward travel evidence may be requested.

Business or events

  • Invitation or letter from the Colombian company, organizer, or institution.
  • Details of the event, meetings, or business activity and the expected dates.
  • Evidence that the activity is permitted under the requested visitor category.

Medical treatment

  • Letter from the Colombian medical provider describing the treatment and estimated duration.
  • Proof of financial capacity or payment arrangements for the treatment and stay.
  • Health coverage or other support documents when requested.

Digital nomad

  • Letter or contract from a foreign company, or contracts proving independent work for clients outside Colombia.
  • Proof of average income equivalent to at least three Colombian minimum monthly wages over the previous three months.
  • Health insurance covering Colombia for the intended stay.
  • The activity must be for foreign clients or companies; this route does not authorize local employment.

Migrant Visa (Type M)

Worker

  • Employer-signed contract summary using the Ministry's required format.
  • Employer motivation letter describing the role, qualifications, duties, and reason for hiring the foreign worker.
  • Employer financial records and evidence of the applicant's education or professional experience.
  • Professional license or authorization when the occupation is regulated.

Spouse or domestic partner

  • Marriage certificate or declaration of a qualifying permanent partnership.
  • Colombian spouse or partner identification and supporting evidence of the relationship.
  • Documents must meet apostille, legalization, and Spanish translation requirements when applicable.

Student

  • Admission or enrollment letter from an authorized Colombian educational institution.
  • Program, duration, and study-load information.
  • Proof of financial support and health coverage when required.

Freelancer

  • Contracts or service agreements showing the professional activity and clients.
  • Evidence of qualifications, professional experience, and sufficient funds.
  • Professional authorization when the activity is regulated in Colombia.

Andean nationality

  • Valid passport or identity document accepted under the applicable Andean rules.
  • Evidence of nationality, clean immigration status, and the purpose of the stay.
  • Additional civil, financial, or background documents requested for the individual case.

Resident Visa (Type R)

Investor

  • Evidence of the qualifying investment made through the authorized Colombian financial system.
  • Documents proving the source, ownership, and registration of the investment.
  • Current tax, corporate, or property records when relevant to the investment type.

Parent of a Colombian national

  • Colombian child's civil birth registration.
  • Parent's identity documents and evidence of the parent-child relationship.
  • Additional documents requested to verify the Colombian nationality and family relationship.

Accumulated time

  • Copies of prior Colombian visas and entry or exit records.
  • Evidence that the applicant meets the required qualifying time under the relevant migrant pathway.
  • Current passport, application records, and supporting documents for the underlying visa history.
