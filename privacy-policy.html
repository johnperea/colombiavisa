1. Who we are

ColombiaVisa (colombiavisa.company) is operated by John Alexandre Perea Bermúdez, a natural person based in Santa Marta, Colombia. Email: john@colombiavisa.company. Phone: +57 300 550 3069. We are the party responsible for your personal data under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012.

2. Information we collect

Contact details (name, email, phone, country), your visa objective and immigration status, the documents you choose to send us, your messages, and technical data needed to operate, secure, and understand use of the website. If Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled, it may receive limited, aggregated technical information such as page visits, referrers, device or browser details, and approximate location. We do not store card numbers; payments are handled by payment providers.

3. How we use it

To answer inquiries, prepare quotes, deliver the advisory, document-review or application services you request, manage payments and records, secure the website, improve its use, and meet legal obligations. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing without your consent.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with website hosting, network and security providers, including Cloudflare Web Analytics when enabled; HubSpot for the contact form and contact management; our email provider; payment providers and banks; authorities when required by law or when you ask us to file an application; and professionals such as translators or lawyers, only with your authorization.

5. International transfers

Some providers store data outside Colombia. By sending us your information you authorize these transfers, which we limit to what the service requires and make only to providers that apply reasonable safeguards. Cloudflare Web Analytics is used to measure website usage with limited, aggregated information when enabled.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only the cookies and similar technologies required by the website, hosting, security, and HubSpot contact form as they are enabled. If Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled, it is used for privacy-conscious, aggregated traffic measurement and is not used to create advertising profiles. We do not claim that advertising, remarketing, or Google reCAPTCHA are active unless those tools are actually configured. If a technology requiring consent is enabled, we will provide the applicable notice and consent mechanism. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some features may stop working.

7. Sensitive data and minors

Provide only what the service needs. If a case requires sensitive data, supplying it is optional and we process it only with your explicit authorization. We process data of minors only when provided by a parent or legal guardian.

8. Retention and security

Identity and supporting documents are deleted within 12 months after your case closes. Inquiries that do not become a service are deleted within 12 months. Payment and accounting records are kept for the period required by law. Please do not send passports or financial records until we confirm a secure delivery method. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission is completely secure.

9. Your rights and contact

You may access, update, correct, request deletion of your data, and revoke your authorization. The procedure and response times are in our Personal Data Processing Policy. Write to john@colombiavisa.company. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
