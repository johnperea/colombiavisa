2. Legal framework

This policy follows Statutory Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015) and related rules on personal data protection in Colombia.

3. Purposes

Answering requests; providing visa advisory, document-review and application services; communicating with applicants; managing payments and records; preventing misuse of the website; measuring and improving website use through privacy-conscious Cloudflare Web Analytics when enabled; and meeting legal duties.

4. Authorization

We process your data with your prior, express and informed authorization, given by accepting the consent box on our contact form or by sending us your information after reading this policy. We keep proof of that authorization. You may revoke it at any time, unless we must keep the data to meet a legal or contractual duty.

5. Your rights

You may: know, update and correct your data; request proof of your authorization; be informed about how your data has been used; request deletion or revoke your authorization when the law allows; access your data free of charge; and file a complaint with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC).

6. How to exercise your rights

Email john@colombiavisa.company with your name, contact details, the right you wish to exercise and enough detail to identify your interaction with us. We may ask for reasonable verification of your identity.

Queries are answered within 10 business days, extendable by 5 more business days if we tell you the reason. Claims are answered within 15 business days, extendable by 8 more business days if we tell you the reason. If a claim is incomplete, we will ask you to complete it within 5 days.

7. Complaints to the SIC

Once you have used the query or claim procedure with us, you may file a complaint with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (www.sic.gov.co).

8. Sensitive data and minors

Sensitive data is optional and processed only with your explicit authorization. Data of minors is processed only when provided by a parent or legal guardian. Do not send passports or financial records until we confirm a secure delivery method.

9. International transfers

Providers such as Cloudflare Web Analytics, HubSpot, email and hosting services may store or process data outside Colombia. By authorizing this policy you authorize those transfers, limited to what the service requires. Cloudflare Web Analytics is used for limited, aggregated traffic measurement when enabled.

10. Security and retention

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Identity and supporting documents are deleted within 12 months after your case closes; unconverted inquiries within 12 months; payment and accounting records for the period required by law.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the current version; material changes will be announced on the website.
